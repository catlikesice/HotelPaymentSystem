Welcome to the Blog

Explore the people, places, festivals, and everyday traditions that make Northern Europe special. New stories will appear here as we grow this collection—start with the features below.

Midsummer Along the Baltic Coast

From flower crowns to bonfires by the sea, Midsummer is one of the region’s most beloved celebrations. Discover how Latvia, Estonia, and Lithuania welcome the longest days of the year.

Art Nouveau Walks in Riga

Alberta iela and the surrounding streets hold some of Europe’s finest Art Nouveau façades. A slow walk through this district is an open-air museum of sculpture, symbolism, and city history.

Sauna Culture from Finland to Estonia

Heat, steam, and a plunge into cool water are more than a pastime—they are a shared Northern ritual. Learn why the sauna remains central to hospitality and everyday life across the region.
