# Boreal Horizons — Product Features

## Overview

**Boreal Horizons** is a culturally focused accommodation-booking platform designed to help travellers discover places to stay, understand the destinations they visit, and connect with local experiences and communities.

The platform will initially focus on Scotland and North East England, followed by selected destinations across the Baltics, Scandinavia, the Caucasus, the Balkans, and Switzerland. It combines accommodation booking, editorial travel content, and optional cryptocurrency payments within one service.

> **Project status:** Boreal Horizons is an early-stage product in development. The features below describe the planned product, including the initial minimum viable product (MVP) and later capabilities.

## Core Value Proposition

Boreal Horizons is intended to offer:

- A regional and culturally informed alternative to large, general-purpose booking platforms.
- Accommodation discovery alongside useful editorial content about culture, history, food, nature, and local events.
- Conventional booking information with an optional non-custodial cryptocurrency payment route.
- Opportunities for travellers to discover and join relevant local events or group activities.
- Greater visibility for independent accommodation providers and locally rooted tourism businesses.

## Initial MVP Features

### 1. Accommodation Search and Discovery

Travellers will be able to:

- Search for accommodation by destination.
- Select check-in and check-out dates.
- Specify the number of guests and rooms.
- Browse available properties in supported locations.
- Filter or compare properties using essential criteria such as price, property type, and available facilities.
- View clear property summaries before opening a full listing.

The earliest prototype may use a limited, curated inventory while the property database and partner network are developed.

### 2. Property Listings

Each accommodation page is expected to include:

- Property name, location, and description.
- Photographs and key facilities.
- Available room types.
- Prices and availability.
- Check-in, cancellation, and house policies.
- A map or location reference.
- Payment options supported by the property or platform.
- Links to relevant Boreal Horizons destination content.

### 3. Booking Journey

The booking flow will allow a customer to:

1. Choose a destination and travel dates.
2. Select a property and room.
3. Review the booking price and conditions.
4. Enter the required guest and contact information.
5. Choose an available payment method.
6. Submit the reservation.
7. Receive a booking reference and confirmation.

During the earliest MVP stage, some reservations and confirmations may be supported by manual back-office processes while automated integrations are developed.

### 4. Non-Custodial Cryptocurrency Payments

Boreal Horizons plans to support optional wallet-to-wallet cryptocurrency payments. The intended model is non-custodial:

- Customers retain control of their own wallets and private keys.
- Boreal Horizons does not operate a customer crypto wallet.
- Boreal Horizons does not hold customer cryptocurrency on account.
- A checkout page can display the amount, supported asset, destination wallet address, network, and a QR code.
- The customer authorises and sends the payment using an external self-custody wallet.
- Transaction details can be used to support payment verification and booking confirmation.

Supported assets and networks will be introduced selectively. Payment design and availability will remain subject to technical, commercial, and legal review in each target market.

### 5. Pricing and Booking Transparency

Before confirming a booking, customers should be shown:

- Accommodation price.
- Taxes and mandatory charges, where applicable.
- Boreal Horizons fees, if any.
- The total amount payable.
- The selected currency or crypto asset.
- Cancellation and refund terms.
- Any relevant exchange-rate or network-fee information.

### 6. Booking Management and Customer Support

Customers will be able to access essential booking information, including:

- Booking reference and current status.
- Property and stay details.
- Payment status.
- Cancellation conditions.
- Instructions for requesting changes, cancellations, or support.

The initial service may use email and a customer-support platform for enquiries, with a fuller self-service account area added later.

### 7. Cultural and Destination Blog

Editorial content is a central feature rather than an unrelated addition. The Boreal Horizons blog will cover topics such as:

- Local culture and history.
- Arts, music, literature, and architecture.
- Regional food and drink.
- Hiking, nature, and outdoor travel.
- Neighbourhood and city guides.
- Seasonal events and festivals.
- Practical and responsible travel advice.
- Independent local businesses and community initiatives.

Articles can link directly to relevant destinations and accommodation listings, helping readers move naturally from inspiration to trip planning.

### 8. Responsive and Accessible Experience

The product will be designed for desktop and mobile use, with attention to:

- Clear navigation and readable content.
- Responsive page layouts.
- Keyboard-friendly interaction.
- Descriptive form labels and image text alternatives.
- Understandable validation and error messages.
- Efficient pages for travellers using limited or mobile data.

### 9. Essential Trust, Privacy, and Policy Pages

The MVP will include clear access to:

- Terms and conditions.
- Privacy information.
- Cookie information and controls where required.
- Booking, cancellation, and refund policies.
- Payment explanations and risk notices.
- Contact and customer-support information.

## Planned Post-MVP Features

### 10. Customer Accounts

Registered users may be able to:

- View current and previous bookings.
- Save favourite properties and destinations.
- Store travel preferences.
- Manage contact and profile details.
- Receive personalised destination and editorial recommendations.

Guest checkout should remain available where practical.

### 11. Accommodation Partner Portal

An extranet or partner dashboard is planned to allow accommodation providers to:

- Create and update property listings.
- Manage photographs, descriptions, facilities, and policies.
- Update room inventory, prices, and availability.
- Review reservations and booking details.
- Respond to cancellation or change requests.
- Access payment and commission records.
- View basic performance information.

### 12. Events and Social Travel

A later release is intended to help travellers discover activities and meet people while visiting a destination. Possible capabilities include:

- Browsing local events and organised group activities.
- Joining an event associated with a city or travel date.
- Reserving or paying for eligible activities online.
- Viewing organiser information, schedules, capacity, and meeting points.
- Connecting accommodation bookings with relevant local experiences.

This feature will be built with appropriate privacy, moderation, safety, and organiser-verification controls.

### 13. Multilingual Content

Boreal Horizons is intended to support multiple languages as the platform expands. Planned capabilities include:

- Language selection across the main interface.
- Translated destination and editorial content.
- Localised prices, dates, and booking information.
- A translation workflow that permits human review of important commercial and policy text.

Likely early languages include English, German, Polish, and Russian, with further languages introduced according to regional demand.

### 14. Expanded Search and Personalisation

Later search improvements may include:

- Autocomplete for cities, regions, and landmarks.
- Map-based browsing.
- Filters for accessibility, sustainability, remote work, family travel, and outdoor activities.
- Recommendations informed by interests and previous activity.
- Curated collections linked to blog themes or seasonal campaigns.

### 15. Reviews and Reputation

A verified-review system may allow customers who completed a stay to provide feedback about:

- Accommodation quality.
- Cleanliness and facilities.
- Location.
- Service and communication.
- Value for money.

Review moderation and anti-fraud measures will be required before this feature is released.

### 16. Refund and Cancellation Workflows

Automated workflows are planned for:

- Customer cancellation requests.
- Property-approved changes.
- Refund eligibility calculations.
- Refund status tracking.
- Recording fiat or crypto refund transactions.

Because cryptocurrency transfers are generally irreversible, crypto refunds would normally require a separate authorised transaction to a customer-supplied wallet address, with safeguards to verify the request and destination.

### 17. Partner and Tourism Integrations

Future integrations may include:

- Property-management and channel-management systems.
- Availability and booking APIs.
- Third-party payment providers.
- Tourism boards and destination-marketing organisations.
- Event organisers and local experience providers.
- Mapping, analytics, email, and customer-support services.

## Geographic Rollout

The proposed rollout is regional and phased:

1. **Initial market:** Scotland and North East England.
2. **Early international expansion:** Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Denmark, Finland, Norway, and Sweden.
3. **Further target regions:** Georgia and Armenia; Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, and Montenegro; and Switzerland.

Actual launch order will depend on accommodation partnerships, customer demand, operational readiness, and country-specific legal requirements.

## Product Principles

Boreal Horizons will be developed around the following principles:

- **Culture-led discovery:** destinations should be presented as living places, not merely collections of rooms.
- **Customer choice:** crypto should be an optional payment route, not a barrier to using the platform.
- **Non-custodial design:** customers should retain control of their external wallets and private keys.
- **Transparent booking:** prices, conditions, payment status, and cancellation terms should be easy to understand.
- **Regional relevance:** content and partnerships should reflect local knowledge and independent businesses.
- **Responsible growth:** geographic and technical expansion should follow operational and regulatory readiness.
- **Privacy and security by design:** only necessary data should be collected, protected, and retained.
- **Accessible travel technology:** the service should be usable across devices, connection speeds, and a range of access needs.

## Important Note

This document is a product overview and roadmap. It does not constitute a promise that every planned feature will be available at launch. Features, supported locations, payment methods, and release priorities may change as Boreal Horizons completes user research, partnership development, technical testing, and regulatory review.
