Visit Northern Europe

Northern Europe—The Baltic States, Denmark, Sweden, Scotland, Iceland, the Faroe Islands, Greenland, the Åland Islands, and Svalbard offer a captivating blend of history, culture, and natural beauty. Whether you're an adventurer, history buff, foodie, or want to try something new, Northern Europe promises something special.

Unforgettable Sights in Tallinn, Estonia

  • Medieval Old Town: Wander the cobbled streets of the UNESCO-listed Old Town, complete with preserved medieval walls and fairy-tale towers.
  • Toompea Castle & Alexander Nevsky Cathedral: Explore Estonia’s seat of power and admire the ornate Russian Orthodox cathedral.
  • Seaplane Harbour: One of Europe’s best maritime museums, boasting historic ships and interactive exhibits.
  • Local Cafés & Vibrant Nightlife: Enjoy cozy cafés, lively bars, and sample craft beer in eclectic settings.

Must-See Attractions in Riga, Latvia

  • Art Nouveau District: Stroll Alberta iela to see some of the world’s most magnificent art nouveau architecture.
  • Riga Old Town: Get lost in cobblestone lanes, charming squares, and striking landmarks like the House of the Blackheads.
  • Central Market: Discover Europe’s largest market housed in old Zeppelin hangars—perfect for sampling regional delicacies.
  • Museum of the Occupation of Latvia: Gain insights into Latvia’s complex past through engaging exhibits.

Highlights of Copenhagen, Denmark

  • Nyhavn Harbour: Stroll along the colourful waterfront, lined with historic ships, cafés, and classic Danish townhouses.
  • Tivoli Gardens: Experience one of the world’s oldest amusement parks, with fairy-tale lights, gardens, and rides in the heart of the city.
  • The Little Mermaid & Harbour Promenade: Visit Copenhagen’s iconic statue and enjoy scenic walks along the waterfront toward the royal palaces.
  • Nordic Cuisine & Design: Taste New Nordic flavours, browse design shops, and soak up the city’s easy-going hygge atmosphere.

Must-See Attractions in Edinburgh, Scotland

  • Edinburgh Castle: Explore the fortress crowning Castle Rock, home to the Crown Jewels and sweeping views over the city.
  • The Royal Mile: Walk the historic spine between the castle and Holyrood Palace through closes, wynds, and lively street scenes.
  • Arthur’s Seat: Hike an ancient volcanic hill for panoramic views of Edinburgh, the Firth of Forth, and the surrounding countryside.
  • Old Town & New Town: Discover UNESCO-listed contrasts—medieval lanes on one side, elegant Georgian streets on the other.

Unforgettable Sights in Vilnius, Lithuania

  • Baroque Old Town: Wander one of Europe’s largest baroque old towns, with courtyards, churches, and cobbled lanes full of character.
  • Cathedral Square & Gediminas Tower: Stand at the heart of the city, then climb for views across Vilnius’s rooftops and the Neris River.
  • Užupis Artists’ Republic: Explore the creative neighbourhood famous for its tongue-in-cheek constitution, galleries, and riverside cafés.
  • Gates of Dawn: Visit the historic city gate chapel, a beloved pilgrimage site with a celebrated icon of the Virgin Mary.

The Beauty of the Scottish Highlands

  • Dramatic Glens & Mountains: Travel through misty valleys and rugged peaks where lochs mirror the sky and every turn feels cinematic.
  • Loch Ness & Great Glen: Follow the Great Glen’s chain of lochs, from legendary Loch Ness to quiet shores perfect for slow exploration.
  • Isle of Skye: Discover jagged ridges, sea cliffs, and fairy-tale landscapes that make Skye one of Scotland’s most unforgettable islands.
  • Castles & Quiet Villages: Find ruined fortresses, whisky distilleries, and welcoming Highland towns set against open skies and wild countryside.

The Scandinavian Mountains in Sweden

  • The Scandinavian Mountains (Scandes): Follow Sweden’s long mountain spine toward the Norwegian border, where alpine peaks, glaciers, and wide valleys shape the far north.
  • Kebnekaise & Lapland Trails: Hike toward Sweden’s highest peak and explore classic routes through Sámi landscapes, mountain huts, and midnight-summer light.
  • Abisko National Park: Watch the Northern Lights in one of Sweden’s clearest night skies, or trek the start of the famous Kungsleden trail.
  • Fjäll Adventures Year-Round: Ski remote slopes in winter, then return in summer for wildflowers, reindeer country, and crystal-clear mountain lakes.

The Curonian Spit in Lithuania

  • UNESCO Sand Dune Peninsula: Walk a slender strip of land between the Baltic Sea and the Curonian Lagoon, where wind-shaped dunes rise above pine forests.
  • Nida & the Great Dune: Visit the charming resort village of Nida and climb the shifting sands for sweeping views over sea, lagoon, and forest.
  • Lagoon Villages & Fishing Heritage: Explore wooden cottages, traditional weather vanes, and quiet harbours that still reflect the spit’s seafaring past.
  • Wildlife & Coastal Paths: Spot migrating birds, cycle forested trails, and feel the unique calm of one of Europe’s most delicate coastal landscapes.

The Åland Islands

  • Mariehamn Harbour: Arrive in Åland’s small capital by ferry, then wander the western harbour, maritime museum, and the historic barque Pommern.
  • Kastelholm & Bomarsund: Visit a medieval castle and the ruined Russian fortress that still mark the islands’ layered Swedish, Russian, and autonomous past.
  • Red Granite Archipelago: Island-hop among smooth skerries, wooden jetties, and quiet swimming coves in the Baltic’s most sheltered inner waters.
  • Swedish-Speaking Island Life: Taste Åland pancakes with stewed plums, cycle between red granite farms, and enjoy a bilingual Nordic culture of its own.

Greenland

  • Nuuk, the Capital on the Fjord: Explore colourful harbour houses, the National Museum, and mountain trails that start almost from the edge of town.
  • Ilulissat Icefjord: Watch icebergs calve from Sermeq Kujalleq, one of the world’s most active glaciers, in a UNESCO-listed Arctic landscape.
  • Midnight Sun & Northern Lights: Travel in summer for endless daylight on the water, or in winter for aurora nights above Disko Bay and Nuuk Fjord.
  • Inuit Culture & Arctic Food: Hear drum dancing and storytelling, then try Greenlandic flavours from freshly landed fish to wild-harvested ingredients.

The Faroe Islands

  • Tórshavn & Tinganes: Wander the grass-roofed parliament quarter and the compact harbour capital that has been a North Atlantic meeting place for centuries.
  • Sea Cliffs & Turf-Roof Villages: Follow roads and footpaths to villages such as Gjógv and Saksun, where waterfalls drop toward the ocean and turf roofs blend into the hills.
  • Island-Hopping by Tunnel & Ferry: Cross under-sea tunnels and short ferry hops to reach Vágar, Streymoy, Eysturoy, and the remote northern isles in a single trip.
  • Bird Cliffs & Hiking: Watch puffins on Mykines or hike the ridge above Sørvágsvatn, where a lake appears to hover above the Atlantic.

Svalbard

  • Longyearbyen: Stay in the colourful High Arctic town at 78° north, then visit the Svalbard Museum, the harbour, and the hillside Global Seed Vault.
  • Glaciers, Fjords & Wildlife: Join a guided boat or snowmobile trip across Isfjorden and the plateau, watching for reindeer, Arctic foxes, walrus, and—at a safe distance—polar bears.
  • Midnight Sun & Polar Night: Travel in summer for 24-hour daylight on the ice, or in winter for aurora nights above Adventfjorden and the dark-sky valleys around town.
  • Mining Towns & Polar Research: Day-trip to historic Pyramiden and Barentsburg, or learn how Ny-Ålesund still hosts one of the world’s northernmost research communities.

Why Visit Northern Europe?

  • Beautiful Coastlines: The Baltic Sea offers sandy beaches, picturesque islands, and fresh sea air.
  • Outdoor Adventures: Hiking in national parks, cycling through forests, or relaxing in rural countryside villages.
  • Festivals & Events: Experience vibrant folk festivals, open-air concerts, and magical Christmas markets.
